## Submission Angle

Lead with this:

> ReliefGrid turns scattered emergency signals into a ranked dispatch brief, so local teams know which zone gets scarce supplies first and why.

Do not frame it as a generic dashboard. The strongest angle is transparent crisis prioritization plus an operational output.

## Rubric Mapping

### Innovation & Creativity, 25%

ReliefGrid is a small emergency coordination tool focused on the decision gap between reports and action. The dispatch brief is the differentiator.

### Technical Implementation, 25%

Working static prototype with editable zone signals, transparent scoring, scenario loading, canvas response map, localStorage save, clipboard flow, PNG export, and local WebM demo generation.

### Real-World Impact & Scalability, 25%

Targets heatwaves, floods, clinic outages, and community resource shortages. It is intentionally local-first so it can still be useful when accounts, cloud tools, or stable connectivity are unavailable.

### Design, Presentation & UX, 25%

The first screen is the actual operating surface. Judges see the top mission, route map, response zones, and first-hour plan without reading a long explanation first.

## Main Risk

The hackathon asks for GitHub, demo video, pitch deck, and screenshots. The local package is ready, but the external GitHub/video/live links still need to be created by the user.
